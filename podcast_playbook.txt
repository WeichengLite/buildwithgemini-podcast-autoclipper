Podcast Post-Production Playbook & Production Guidelines

1. Host Introduction Voice & Style:
- Always start with an energetic and direct hook sentence that poses a compelling question or highlight.
- Keep host intros concise (2-3 sentences max) before introducing the guest and topic.
- Tone should be professional yet warm, high-signal, and tech-forward.

2. Podcast Auto-Clipping Standards:
- Focus on moments that contain surprising insights, direct predictions, or actionable technical advice.
- Optimal promo clip length: 30 to 60 seconds.
- Chapter timestamps must be formatted cleanly as (xx:xx)subtitle.

3. Spotify Intro Page Requirements:
- Title must feature guest name formatted as: "Title (feat. Guest Name)".
- First sentence must grab attention immediately using active verbs.
- Include a 2-sentence episode overview summarizing key takeaways.
- Include complete timeline formatted strictly as (xx:xx)subtitle.

4. SEO & Social Distribution Guidelines:
- Primary SEO tags: AI, MachineLearning, Podcast, SoftwareEngineering, Technology.
- Always include direct links to public assets (cover art image and MP3 highlight clip).
- Always include an Apple & Spotify compliant RSS 2.0 item feed export.
